# Phase 01 - Brand Direction and Final Copy Outline

## Brand Positioning (Locked)
- Brand: **Hometown**
- Core market: Kansas City local service businesses
- Promise: premium-looking websites at small-business-friendly pricing
- Primary outcome: more qualified local inquiries
- Tone: confident, direct, modern, local, trustworthy

## Offer Framework (Locked)
- Primary package: **Custom Website Package — $800 this month / $999 regular**
- Secondary offers:
  - Google Business Profile Setup — $250
  - Logo Design + Mini Brand Kit — $250
  - Social Media Management — $499/month
- Primary CTA label: **Get Started**

## Message Pillars
1. Local and easy to work with (Kansas City grounded)
2. Senior-level visual quality without inflated agency pricing
3. Built for lead flow and conversion clarity
4. Simple process for busy small business owners
