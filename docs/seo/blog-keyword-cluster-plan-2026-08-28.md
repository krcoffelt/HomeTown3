# HometownKC Five-Post Keyword Cluster Plan

Planning date: 2026-08-28

Goal: publish five useful, lead-relevant articles that can capture broader informational demand while strengthening Hometown's canonical website-design, SEO, paid-ads, and service-area pages.

## Demand Method and Guardrails

The workspace does not include a current Google Keyword Planner or Google Search Console query export, so this plan does not invent exact monthly search volumes. Demand is graded comparatively:

- **High**: a broad, established national query family with many current exact-intent results, plus a natural Kansas City variation.
- **Medium-high**: an established query family with narrower commercial or problem-solving intent.
- **Local qualification**: the national head term supplies the larger search pool; Kansas City, metro, and city examples qualify readers and connect the article to Hometown's actual service area.

Before drafting, validate each full cluster in Google Keyword Planner with the Kansas City DMA selected. Keep a post in the queue when either:

1. the Kansas City cluster totals at least 100 average monthly searches, or
2. the national head-term cluster totals at least 500 average monthly searches and Google Search Console shows relevant Kansas City impressions.

Do not turn every city modifier into a separate blog post. One strong metro-wide article should use selected local examples and link to the canonical location pages. Commercial phrases already mapped in `docs/seo/keyword-monitoring.csv` stay with their existing service or location URLs.

## Shared Content Requirements

- Aim for 1,500–2,200 useful words, but let the query determine the final length.
- Put a direct 40–60 word answer under the H1, then provide a jump-linked table of contents.
- Use Kansas City naturally in the title, introduction, one local-example section, and conclusion. Do not repeat every city name in headings.
- Include one original decision table or downloadable checklist so each post is more useful than a generic article.
- Link to one primary service page, two or three contextually relevant location/proof pages, and `/contact#form`.
- Add BlogPosting schema and keep any FAQ schema aligned with visible copy.
- Use original screenshots, diagrams, or approved project imagery with descriptive alt text.
- Do not publish fixed Hometown service prices or unsupported performance claims.

## Measurement Plan

Record a baseline on publication day, then review at 28, 56, and 90 days:

- Google Search Console impressions, clicks, CTR, and average position for the full cluster.
- Kansas City and city-modified queries that appear even when they were not in the initial list.
- Internal clicks from the article to service, location, work, and contact pages.
- Form starts, completed forms, phone clicks, and qualified leads from the article landing session.
- Cannibalization: if the article replaces a service page for a commercial query, adjust title/internal anchors so the service page remains the commercial target.
